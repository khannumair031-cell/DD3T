Android Low-Latency & Export Guide

1. Best Live Monitoring Setup on Android

  1. Connect wired 3.5mm or USB-C earphones/microphone for the lowest practical monitoring latency and natural wideband voice.
  2. Keep Wired Mic / Earphone Mode selected so Android bypasses narrowband phone-call processing.
  3. Bluetooth earphones add 100–250 ms wireless delay and are not recommended for live monitoring.
  4. When using the phone's built-in speaker without earphones, switch to Normal (Speaker Safe) mode to prevent acoustic feedback.

2. Where Exported WAV Recordings Are Saved

  1. Tap Record to capture your voice or imported audio through the DD-3T delay pedal.
  2. Tap Stop to finalize, Listen to preview, and Export WAV to save a 16-bit stereo WAV file.
  3. Saved recordings are stored directly in your device's Downloads/BOSS-DD3T/ folder.
